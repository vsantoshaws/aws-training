Document: Exchange Policy

EXCHANGE POLICY OVERVIEW

Metro Grand Mall allows exchanges for a different size, color, or model of the same item,
or for a different item of equal or lesser value, within the same return windows described
in the Return Policy document (30 days standard, 15 days for electronics, 14 days for
beauty products, 45 days for furniture).

EXCHANGE VS. RETURN

- An exchange swaps your item for a different size, color, style, or a different product,
  without you having to wait for a refund to be issued first.
- If the replacement item costs more than the original, you will be charged the
  difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded to your original payment method.
- If you'd rather receive money back than a new item, request a "return for refund"
  instead of an exchange.

SIZE AND COLOR EXCHANGES (APPAREL AND FOOTWEAR)

1. Bring the item, original tags, and proof of purchase to the store where it was
   purchased, or any Metro Grand Mall Customer Service desk.
2. Subject to availability, you may swap for a different size or color of the same
   item at no additional cost.
3. If the desired size/color is out of stock in-store, associates can check inventory
   at other Metro Grand Mall locations or arrange a ship-to-home exchange at no extra
   shipping charge.

ELECTRONICS EXCHANGES

- Defective electronics may be exchanged for an identical replacement within 15 days of
  purchase, no questions asked, provided the defect is confirmed by a quick in-store
  diagnostic check.
- Non-defective electronics ("changed my mind") exchanges are treated as a return
  followed by a new purchase, and must meet the unopened/original-packaging condition
  requirements in the Return Policy.
- Exchanges of open-box electronics for a different model may incur a 10% restocking
  fee on the original item, waived for Metro Grand Mall Rewards Platinum members.

ONLINE MARKETPLACE EXCHANGES

1. Go to "My Orders" in the Metro Grand Mall app or website.
2. Select "Exchange Item" (only available for eligible categories: apparel, footwear,
   and accessories).
3. Choose the new size or color; a prepaid return label for the original item will be
   emailed to you.
4. The replacement item ships automatically once the original item's return label has
   been scanned by the carrier; you do not need to wait for the original item to arrive
   back at the warehouse.
5. If the exchange request is for a different product entirely (not just size/color),
   it must be processed as a standard return and a new order.

GIFT EXCHANGES

Items received as gifts can be exchanged using the gift receipt, without revealing the
original purchase price to the recipient. Gift exchanges can be for a different size,
color, or a different item; price differences are handled via store credit rather than
a refund to a card, unless the gift-giver's original payment method is provided.

ITEMS NOT ELIGIBLE FOR EXCHANGE

The same categories excluded from returns are also excluded from exchanges: gift cards,
opened software/digital downloads, intimate apparel and swimwear without an intact liner,
pierced jewelry, perishable goods, and items marked "Final Sale."

CONTACT

For help with an exchange, visit the Customer Service desk on Level 1, call
1-800-555-0199, or use the "Start an Exchange" option in the Metro Grand Mall app chat.
