METRO GRAND MALL - CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: General FAQ
Last Updated: 2026-01-15

Q: What are Metro Grand Mall's opening hours?
A: Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM. The food court stays
open one hour later than the rest of the mall every day. Hours may vary on public
holidays; check the "Hours & Holidays" page in the app for exceptions.

Q: Do I need a receipt to return or exchange an item?
A: A receipt, gift receipt, or digital proof of purchase (order confirmation email or
in-app purchase history) is required for returns and exchanges. Without any proof of
purchase, store management may offer store credit at the item's current selling price,
at their discretion, and may decline the return entirely for high-value items.

Q: Can I return an online marketplace purchase to a physical store?
A: Yes. Bring the item and your online order confirmation (printed or on your phone) to
any Customer Service desk. Store associates can process most online returns in-store,
except for furniture and large appliances that require scheduled pickup.

Q: How long do refunds take to process?
A: 3-5 business days to a card, instantly to a Metro Grand Mall Rewards balance, and up
to 10 business days for a mailed check on large cash refunds. See the Refund Policy
document for full details by payment method.

Q: What is the difference between "Final Sale" and regular clearance items?
A: "Final Sale" items cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances except
manufacturer defects. Regular clearance items follow the standard Return Policy return
window, just at their marked-down clearance price.

Q: Can I get a price adjustment if an item goes on sale after I bought it?
A: Yes, within 7 days of purchase, Metro Grand Mall will refund the price difference if
the identical item goes on sale, either in-store or on the online marketplace. Bring your
receipt to the Customer Service desk or request the adjustment through the app chat.

Q: Do you price match other retailers?
A: Metro Grand Mall price-matches identical, in-stock items sold by the manufacturer's
official online store, subject to verification. Price matching does not apply to
flash sales, clearance, or limited-time doorbuster promotions.

Q: How do I join Metro Grand Mall Rewards?
A: Sign up for free in the Metro Grand Mall app or at any Customer Service desk. Members
earn 2 points per dollar spent, get free return shipping on online orders, and receive
early access to sales events.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Metro
Grand Mall gift cards, and Metro Grand Mall Buy Now Pay Later installment plans.

Q: Is gift wrapping available?
A: Yes, gift wrapping is available at the Customer Service desk on Level 1 for a small
fee ($3.99), and free for purchases over $75 during the November-December holiday season.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: Visit the Customer Service desk (Level 1, North Entrance), call 1-800-555-0199 (daily,
9 AM - 8 PM), email support@metrograndmall.example, or chat with the virtual assistant in
the Metro Grand Mall app, available 24/7.

Q: Where is parking, and is it free?
A: Parking is free for the first 3 hours in the North, South, and East parking structures.
Validate your parking ticket at any store or the Customer Service desk for up to 2
additional free hours.
