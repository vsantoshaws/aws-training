REFUND POLICY OVERVIEW

Once a return is received and approved (in-store or via the online marketplace), Metro
Grand Mall issues refunds according to the rules below. Refunds are always issued to the
original form of payment unless stated otherwise.

REFUND METHODS AND TIMING

- Credit or debit card: 3-5 business days after the return is processed. The exact
  posting time depends on your card issuer.
- Metro Grand Mall Rewards account balance: Instant, available immediately for use
  in-store or online.
- Cash purchases: Refunded in cash at the Customer Service desk on Level 1, up to $500.
  Cash refunds over $500 are issued as a mailed check within 10 business days.
- PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay purchases: 3-5 business days, refunded to the
  original digital wallet.
- Gift card purchases: Refunded to a new Metro Grand Mall gift card, not to cash or a
  credit card.
- Buy Now Pay Later (installment) purchases: The refund is applied to your installment
  plan balance first; any remaining amount is refunded to your original payment method.

PARTIAL REFUNDS

Partial refunds may apply when:
- An item is returned without original packaging or accessories (up to a 20% deduction).
- A restocking fee applies (electronics opened for a non-defective exchange/return: 10%;
  furniture requiring pickup: flat $49 fee deducted from the refund).
- Only part of a multi-item order is being returned; only the returned items' value is
  refunded, and any bundle discount is recalculated proportionally.

SHIPPING COST REFUNDS

- Original outbound shipping charges are refunded only if the item was defective,
  damaged in transit, or the wrong item was shipped.
- For "changed my mind" returns, original outbound shipping fees are non-refundable, and
  a return shipping fee of $6.99 is deducted from the refund unless you are a Metro Grand
  Mall Rewards Platinum member (free return shipping).

WHEN REFUNDS ARE ISSUED

- In-store returns: Refund is initiated immediately once the item passes inspection at
  the Customer Service desk or store register.
- Online marketplace returns: Refund is initiated within 2 business days of the returned
  package arriving at the fulfillment center, following a quality inspection.
- Curbside/home pickup returns (furniture, appliances): Refund is initiated within 5
  business days of the pickup being completed and the item being inspected.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEM REFUNDS

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or not as described, Metro Grand Mall covers full
return shipping and issues a complete refund, including original shipping charges, with
no restocking fee. Please report damaged or defective items within 7 days of delivery for
the fastest resolution; report using the "Report a Problem" option in the Metro Grand
Mall app or by calling 1-800-555-0199.

REFUND STATUS TRACKING

You can check the status of a refund at any time by visiting "My Orders" > "Returns &
Refunds" in the Metro Grand Mall app, or by asking a Customer Service associate for a
return receipt number lookup.

DISPUTED OR MISSING REFUNDS

If a refund has not appeared within the timeframes above, contact Metro Grand Mall
Support at 1-800-555-0199 or support@metrograndmall.example with your order number and
return confirmation number. Most refund discrepancies are resolved within 3 business
days of being reported.
