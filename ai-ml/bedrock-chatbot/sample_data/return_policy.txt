RETURN POLICY OVERVIEW

Metro Grand Mall wants every purchase to be a great experience. If something isn't right,
most items purchased from any store inside Metro Grand Mall (in-store or via the Metro Grand
Mall online marketplace) can be returned within 30 days of the original purchase date.

STANDARD RETURN WINDOW

- General merchandise (clothing, accessories, home goods, toys, books): 30 days from the
  date of purchase, as shown on the original receipt or order confirmation email.
- Electronics and appliances: 15 days from the date of purchase. The item must include all
  original accessories, cables, manuals, and packaging.
- Beauty and personal care items: 14 days, and only if unopened and unused with the safety
  seal intact.
- Furniture and mattresses: 45 days, but a $49 return pickup fee applies for items requiring
  home pickup.
- Perishable goods (food court items, fresh flowers, plants): No returns, all sales final.
- Custom or personalized items (engraved jewelry, monogrammed goods, made-to-order
  furniture): No returns unless the item arrives damaged or defective.

CONDITION REQUIREMENTS

To be eligible for a return, items generally must be:
1. In original, unused condition with tags attached.
2. In the original packaging where applicable.
3. Accompanied by the original receipt, gift receipt, or digital proof of purchase
   (order confirmation email or the Metro Grand Mall app purchase history).

Items that are worn, altered, damaged by the customer, or missing original tags may be
denied a return or receive a reduced refund at the store manager's discretion.

HOW TO RETURN AN ITEM

In-Store Returns:
1. Bring the item and proof of purchase to any Metro Grand Mall Customer Service desk
   (Level 1, near the North Entrance) or directly to the store where it was purchased.
2. A customer service associate will inspect the item and process the return.
3. Refunds to a credit or debit card typically post within 3-5 business days.

Online Marketplace Returns:
1. Log in to the Metro Grand Mall app or website and go to "My Orders."
2. Select the item you want to return and choose a reason for the return.
3. Print the prepaid return label (free for defective items; a $6.99 return shipping fee
   is deducted from the refund for other return reasons).
4. Drop the package at any shipping carrier location listed in the return instructions.
5. Once the returned item is received and inspected (usually within 5-7 business days),
   your refund will be issued to the original payment method.

ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE RETURNED

- Gift cards and Metro Grand Mall mall gift cards
- Opened software, video games, or digital downloads
- Undergarments, swimwear (unless the hygiene liner is intact), and pierced jewelry
- Items marked "Final Sale" or purchased from the clearance/outlet section
- Prescription eyewear and hearing aids (see store-specific policy)

LATE RETURNS

Returns requested after the applicable return window may be offered store credit at the
current selling price, at the discretion of store management, but this is not guaranteed.

CONTACT

For questions about a specific return, visit the Customer Service desk on Level 1, call
the Metro Grand Mall Support Line at 1-800-555-0199, or chat with our virtual assistant
in the Metro Grand Mall app.
