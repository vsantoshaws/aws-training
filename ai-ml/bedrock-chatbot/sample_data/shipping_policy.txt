METRO GRAND MALL - CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Shipping & Delivery Policy
Last Updated: 2026-01-15

SHIPPING OPTIONS (ONLINE MARKETPLACE ORDERS)

- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days, $5.99, free on orders over $50.
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days, $12.99.
- Next-Day Shipping: 1 business day if ordered before 2 PM local time, $24.99.
- Same-Day Delivery: Available for select stores within 15 miles of Metro Grand Mall,
  $9.99, order must be placed before 12 PM local time.
- In-Store Pickup: Free, ready within 2 hours for in-stock items; you'll receive a
  pickup-ready notification via app, email, or text.
- Curbside Pickup: Free, available at the South Parking Entrance, ready within 2 hours.

FURNITURE AND LARGE-ITEM DELIVERY

- White Glove Delivery (furniture, large appliances, mattresses): $79.99 flat fee,
  includes delivery, assembly, and removal of packaging materials. Delivery window is
  typically 7-14 business days and is scheduled by a delivery coordinator who will call
  you 1-2 days in advance.
- Threshold Delivery (item left at your door or garage): $39.99, 5-10 business days.

ORDER TRACKING

Once an order ships, a tracking number is emailed and available under "My Orders" in the
Metro Grand Mall app. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after a shipping
confirmation is sent.

DELIVERY ISSUES

- Lost packages: If tracking shows "delivered" but the package cannot be located, report
  it within 48 hours via the app's "Report a Problem" flow; Metro Grand Mall will
  investigate with the carrier and issue a replacement or refund within 5 business days.
- Damaged in transit: Refuse the delivery if visibly damaged, or report it within 7 days
  of delivery for a free replacement or full refund including shipping.
- Delayed orders: If a Standard, Express, or Next-Day order has not arrived within 2 days
  of the estimated delivery date, contact Support for a shipping fee refund and updated
  tracking.

INTERNATIONAL AND MULTI-STORE ORDERS

Metro Grand Mall currently ships only within the country of the mall's operation. Orders
containing items from multiple stores inside the mall may arrive in separate shipments
with separate tracking numbers, at no extra shipping cost beyond the single shipping fee
charged at checkout.

CONTACT

For shipping and delivery questions, use the Metro Grand Mall app chat, call
1-800-555-0199, or email support@metrograndmall.example.
